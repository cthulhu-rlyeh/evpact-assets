The pact comes first

Small enough to begin. Bound enough to work.

Eventide Compact / Statement of purpose

EVPACT is a burgeoning idea: a small association taking shape around a promise. We are building the relationships, habits, and practical capacity that make mutual aid dependable. We do not need to be large to be functional. We need to stick to the pact.

Two captains who agree the work, keep the shares, and answer a call honestly can already put that idea into practice. Growth should make the agreement more useful without making it easier to forget.

What we are here to do

We build, recover, trade, and prosper together. Industry gives us useful work. Logistics gets people and supplies where they are needed. Repair and recovery preserve what our crews have earned. Exploration and settlement open room to keep building. Armed protection supports those purposes.

These are the directions we intend to develop. An operation begins with the people, ships, time, and support actually available. We agree its scope before we promise a result.

What the pact asks of us

Keep your word. Agree shares and expenses before departure. Honor contracts and payouts. Be candid about what you can contribute, and give notice when plans change.

Respect other people's independence as you expect them to respect yours. Legitimate claims, applicable laws, and freely accepted obligations have weight. Rank, a corporate seal, or a louder voice does not make an unsupported demand valid.

Help in ways you can sustain: a crew position, a cargo run, a repair, an escort, a recovery, or simply a reliable witness. Ask what happened, keep the evidence, and hear affected parties. Standing beside a member includes holding them accountable.

What we stand against

Piracy, extortion, corruption, and coercion remain the same conduct beneath a uniform or a corporate name. We resist the taking of another person's labor, cargo, or freedom through fabricated claims and convenient assertions of power.

Our own officers are answerable to the same standard. No member may use the Compact to enforce a private shakedown. Force serves protection and recovery, with negotiation or disengagement when workable and proportionate action when necessary.

The gold lining

The black is the void we work in. The gold is the narrow line we choose to keep: sound dealings, solidarity, protected independence, and prosperity earned without becoming the thing we left behind.

Our signatures are in true gold ink because a given word has value. The color is a reminder of the obligation. We still have to live up to it.

Joining the work

Real member expectations

We welcome miners, salvagers, haulers, explorers, builders, engineers, and protection crews who value independence and keep their word. Bring a crew or find one here. Ship ownership is not a requirement; reliable crewmates matter more than a hangar list.

Tell us what you enjoy doing, your experience, usual availability and time zone, and what you would like to contribute or learn. Be clear about commitments, respect fellow players, and honor agreed shares. Casual commitment, coordinated operations, and room for lore and character. Real life comes first.

The charter sets out the obligations behind this statement. The Compact grows each time someone keeps them.

Our word binds us.
