EVENTIDE COMPACT
CHARTER & OPERATING DOCTRINE
OUR WORD BINDS US.

1. Authority must have a basis. Any demand upon a member's vessel, labor, cargo, or earnings may be examined for jurisdiction, scope, and evidence. Rank and urgency alone do not settle the question. Inspection applies equally to Compact officers.

2. Legitimate obligations are honored. Members respect applicable laws, substantiated claims, agreed access conditions, contracts, and debts. Skepticism does not erase a valid obligation or make compliance depend solely on personal preference.

3. Independence is reciprocal. The Compact rejects piracy, extortion, fabricated claims, and seizures undertaken for convenience or profit. Members may not impose upon others the abuses they organized to resist.

4. An agreement freely made is binding. Signatories retain their independence while accepting the Compact's defined duties. Officers exercise delegated authority within its agreed scope and remain accountable for its use.

5. Mutual aid is practical. Assistance may include witnesses, negotiation, freight, supplies, rescue, repair, recovery, escort, or armed defense. Deploy the ships and people the task requires; preserve the capacity to sustain and extract them.

6. Force protects people and legitimate interests. Seek clarification, document demands, and use negotiation or disengagement when workable. Respond decisively to threats when necessary, with force proportionate to protection and recovery. A dispute does not authorize indiscriminate retaliation or opportunistic looting.

7. Membership carries accountability. Aid to a member does not automatically endorse their account or their actions. Examine evidence, hear affected parties, and require restitution or discipline where warranted. No captain may use the Compact to enforce a private shakedown.

8. Prosperity must sustain the agreement. Establish shares and expenses before an operation. Honor payouts and maintain the supplies, repairs, and reserves that make continued independence possible.

Operational cycle: survey and verify access; assemble a suitable crew; produce or recover; transport and trade; maintain or build; protect and extract. Security supports the whole operation. Fleet ownership does not imply every vessel must deploy at once.
